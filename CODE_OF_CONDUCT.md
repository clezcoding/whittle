# Code of Conduct

We want Whittle to be a friendly place to contribute, whatever your experience, background or language.

## Our standard

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- English is the project language; German is welcome in issues too, a translation will be added if needed.

Unacceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who do so repeatedly.

Report problems privately through a [GitHub security advisory draft](https://github.com/clezcoding/whittle/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer [@clezcoding](https://github.com/clezcoding) on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
